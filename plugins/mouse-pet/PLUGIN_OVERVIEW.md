A small pet mouse lives along the bottom edge of the bb window and keeps you company.

## What you get

- A mouse that walks, runs, sits, grooms and sleeps, and turns around at both window edges.
- A click makes it hop, squeak and run the other way.
- It never blocks the interface: only the mouse takes clicks, the rest of the strip lets them through.
- It sits still when your system asks for reduced motion.
- Disable the plugin and the mouse is gone.

## Requirements

None beyond bb itself.

## Privacy

The plugin makes no network requests and stores nothing.
